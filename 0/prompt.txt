What is the EF-Start challenge?

EF-Start is an exceptionally challenging 7-day remote experience designed to accelerate your entrepreneurial career. You will design an ambitious agenda for your week, and then spend 7 days delivering on it. You will be in a cohort of other exceptional future founders who will support and challenge you through your week.

What will I do during the challenge? 

Through the week, you will work on your agenda individually. We have the following group touch-points: 
Kick-off call;
Daily stand-ups;
Upload a short video each day to track your progress;
Provide further evidence of your progress each day;
Upload final presentations. 

In addition, we are interested in how you are able to support and challenge others, and identify exceptional talent. You should allocate roughly 3h over the week to giving feedback to your peers on our platform. You will also be invited to a Discord community for realtime collaboration. 

What should I work on?

We encourage Starters to define their own challenge. It is important that: 
Your challenge is ambitious, yet can feasibly be done in a heroic lift over 7 days. 
It should demonstrate (or help you refine) your edge.
It is on your path. If you do this challenge and we don’t select you, you will have no regrets because the work will make you more likely to succeed anyhow.
